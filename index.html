Formulae used

qu = c·Nc·sc + q·Nq + 0.5·γ·B·Nγ·sγ
  • q = γ·Df (adjusted if the water table is within the influence zone)
  • Strip: sc = sγ = 1. Square: sc = 1.3, sγ = 0.8. Rectangle: sc = 1 + 0.3B/L, sγ = 1 − 0.2B/L
  • Local shear: c′ = ⅔c and tan φ′ = ⅔ tan φ
  • Net safe capacity: qnet,safe = (qu − q)/FS, and qsafe = qnet,safe + q
